Platform FAQ.

Is iNNkie really free to use?

Yes. All of our core utilities (Image compression, PDF extraction, UTM building, and base URL shortening) are completely free. We keep costs low by using your device's power to process files instead of expensive server clusters.

Is it safe to use iNNkie for company documents?

Absolutely. Because we use browser-side processing for all file tools, your company documents never touch our storage. Even if our database was breached, your files wouldn't be there because we never received them in the first place.

What are the benefits of a Pro Account?

While guests can use the tools, a free account unlocks high-fidelity analytics for your links, custom branded styles for QR codes, and a persistent dashboard to manage your digital assets across multiple workspaces.

Does iNNkie sell my browsing data?

No. Our business model is focused on providing a premium utility platform, not on harvesting personal data. We are privacy-first by design and by choice.
