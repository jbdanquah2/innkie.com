Shortener FAQ

Do my links expire?

Never. Links shortened on iNNkie stay active permanently unless you manually delete them from your dashboard.

Is it free to use?

Yes, the basic shortener is completely free. You can shorten links as a guest or create a free account to track analytics.

Can I use my own domain?

Custom domains are supported for Pro workspaces. This allows you to use your own brand name in your short links.

What is a Dynamic Link?

A dynamic link is a short URL that can be redirected to a new destination after it's been shared. It also provides advanced click tracking.
